02
Supply chain

Python · Pandas · SciPy · Tableau

Delivery Failure Lab

Audited 180,519 order items into 65,752 unique orders to evaluate delivery SLAs, identify services to investigate, and model improvement scenarios.

57.31%eligible orders late
$18.08Mlate-order sales exposure
Key findings & methodology +

Audit: one order receives one vote in SLA KPIs. After excluding 2,855 canceled shipments, 36,048 of 62,897 eligible orders were late. Financial totals reconcile before and after aggregation.

Finding: First Class and Second Class had late rates of 100.00% and 79.99%. Standard Class had the most late orders (14,995) and the largest sales exposure ($7.52M). Shipping mode showed the strongest measured association with late status (Cramér’s V = 0.481).

Recommendation: validate premium-service SLA rules and investigate Standard Class for an operational pilot. A 10% relative reduction would mean 3,605 fewer late orders and a 51.58% late rate at fixed eligible volume.

Limits: historical data from Jan 2015 to Jan 2018. Association does not establish cause. Exposure measures sales attached to late orders, not proven loss. The simulator is a planning scenario, not a forecast or an achieved result.

RevoFin — Credit Risk Analysis

Investigated a risk inflection point across 176K+ loan records and tested restructuring scenarios for borrower cash flow.

176K+records analyzed

Joined loan and customer records, built vintage cohorts, and identified a combination of declining income and automated 13.7% interest rates. Debt consolidation held 62.1% of exposure volume.

A Tableau restructuring simulator showed that a 12-month tenor extension with a 1% rate reduction could free about $85 per month in borrower cash flow.
